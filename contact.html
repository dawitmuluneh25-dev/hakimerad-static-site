What happens after you send this

Three steps, and no obligation at any of them. Prefer to talk it through first? Call +251 906 373154 or email info@hakimerad.health.et.

1. We read it against the current scope

We check your modalities, volume and coverage window against what is available today, and separate what is confirmed from what is conditional.

2. You get a written reply

We reply from info@hakimerad.health.et with an indicative schedule, or with the specific questions we still need answered before one can be prepared.

3. If it fits, we scope a pilot

Any pilot is small, and the service agreement, turnaround targets and escalation route are set out in writing before a single study is sent.
