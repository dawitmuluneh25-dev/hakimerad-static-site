Teleradiology · Ethiopia

Remote radiology reporting for hospitals, clinics and imaging centres in Ethiopia

We read the images your facility already produces — X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy and echocardiography — and return a structured report through a secure portal. You do not have to recruit a radiologist to get your images reported — you get access to one, per study. We work with hospitals, clinics, imaging centres and NGOs. We work with institutions, not individual patients.

What the service provides

Remote interpretation and reporting, delivered as a medical-consultancy service. Imaging is performed at your facility; we read and report it.

Published turnaround targets

Emergency and STAT studies 60–120 minutes, urgent studies within 4 hours, routine studies within 24 hours, measured from upload. These are targets, not guarantees.

Designed data-protection controls

Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, two-factor authentication and time-stamped audit logging. No certification or accreditation is claimed.

Coverage through nights and weekends

24/7 platform access with after-hours and emergency cover, for facilities whose emergency department does not close.

Subspecialty reading where available

Neuroradiology, musculoskeletal, chest and cardiac, oncology imaging and breast imaging. Availability is confirmed at enquiry.

How it works

Four steps, using the equipment and workflow your team already has.

1

Your facility acquires the study

Imaging is performed at your site, as it is today. Nothing about your acquisition protocol changes.

2

The study is uploaded

The study is uploaded to our secure portal, with the clinical information that accompanies it.

3

A licensed radiologist reports it

A reporting radiologist interprets the images and produces a structured report. Urgent and emergency studies are prioritised.

4

The report is returned and archived

The report returns to your facility and is archived. Critical findings are notified to your named clinician, with the time of notification recorded.

Modalities in scope

Reporting for the equipment your facility operates. Availability of individual services is confirmed at enquiry.

X-ray

Chest, skeletal, abdominal and trauma radiographs.

CT

Non-contrast and contrast studies, routine and emergency.

MRI

Routine, contrast and advanced sequences.

Ultrasound

Including Doppler where indicated. Proposed service.

Mammography

Reported using BI-RADS structured reporting.

Fluoroscopy

Fluoroscopy and contrast-study reporting. Proposed service.

Echocardiography

Adult and paediatric transthoracic reporting, including Doppler. Proposed service.

Proposed services. Ultrasound, echocardiography and fluoroscopy reporting are conditional on a pre-trained, qualified or certified professional being available at your site to capture images to the appropriate protocol, on adequate image quality, and on a standardised imaging protocol with an approved imaging instrument. Availability is confirmed by an additional enquiry.
