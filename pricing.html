Engagement models

How institutions work with us. Every engagement is quoted for the institution, at enquiry.

Three ways to engage

No price list is published. The modality mix, monthly volume, urgency mix and coverage window decide the schedule, and those differ by facility.

Model 1

Per-study reporting

Quoted per study, by modality and volume

  • For facilities with fluctuating or low reporting volume
  • Routine turnaround target of 24 hours from upload
  • Structured report returned to your facility and archived
  • Volume-based rates for high-throughput sites

Request a schedule

Model 2

After-hours and emergency cover

Quoted per coverage window

  • Nights, weekends and public holidays
  • Emergency and STAT target of 60–120 minutes from upload
  • Urgent studies within 4 hours
  • Critical findings notified to your named clinician, with the time recorded
  • An agreed escalation contact and out-of-hours route are required

Discuss cover

Model 3

Institutional package

Quoted per facility or per facility group

  • For facilities with a predictable monthly reporting volume
  • Multi-facility framework pricing for regional groupings
  • Reporting across all seven modalities in scope
  • Package contents and inclusions set out in the service agreement
  • Pilot arrangements available while scope is being validated

Request the package sheet

What decides your quote

Five inputs. Send all five and the first response is a schedule rather than a question.

Modality mix — which of the seven modalities in scope you operate.
Monthly volume — indicative studies per month, by modality.
Urgency mix — the share of emergency, urgent and routine work.
Coverage window — business hours only, or nights and weekends.
Integration scope — whether the reporting workflow needs to be scoped against an existing PACS, RIS or EMR. Optional, and confirmed at enquiry.
