Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and what happens to it.

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

The short version

HakimeRAD Teleradiology is a pre-launch service of Hakimerad Medical Consultancy. This website collects the details you type into the enquiry form, and it records which page and which campaign link brought you here. It does not collect patient information, and it does not place advertising or tracking cookies on your device.

What we collect

If you submit the enquiry form, we receive what you enter: your organisation and its type, your city, the reporting problem you selected, your name, role, work email and work phone, whether you are authorised to enquire on the organisation's behalf, and any optional volume or priority information you choose to give.

We also record how you reached the site — the campaign parameters in the link you followed, the page that referred you, and the first page you landed on. This is attached to the enquiry so that we can tell which channels produce genuine institutional interest.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and do not want, patient images, radiology reports, or any information that identifies a patient — not through this website, not by email, and not on any messaging or social channel. If you send such material to an address published here, delete it and use the reporting portal instead.

This website does not process payments, does not ask you to enter clinical or health details, and does not require you to create an account.

How it is processed

Enquiry form. Submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, and delivered by email to the team mailbox registered against the site's form key. Web3Forms processes the submission in transit; it does not receive anything beyond the fields you enter.

Measurement. When site measurement is enabled, it uses Google Analytics 4 with consent-mode storage denied. In that configuration no cookie is written and no persistent identifier is kept on your device; the visit is measured in aggregate only. Enquiry field values are never sent to the measurement service — only the fact that a form was started or a submission was confirmed.

Server logs. The hosting provider keeps standard web server logs, including IP address, for security and troubleshooting.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of the enquiry, then deleted. Measurement data is retained in aggregate according to the analytics property's retention setting.

Your choices

Write to info@hakimerad.health.et to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. If your enquiry reached us through the form, tell us the organisation name and the approximate date so we can find it.
