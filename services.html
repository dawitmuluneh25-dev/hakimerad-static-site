Services

Remote interpretation and structured reporting. The imaging is performed at your facility; we read it and return a report.

What we do, and what we do not

HakimeRAD Teleradiology is a reporting service. Your facility acquires the study; we interpret it and return a structured report through a secure portal. We do not scan patients, we do not accept individual patient referrals, and we do not ask for patient images or identifiable patient information over messaging or social channels.

Every engagement begins with your facility profile: the modalities you operate, your indicative monthly volume, your urgency mix and your escalation contact. That profile decides the schedule, the turnaround targets and whether a subspecialty reader is available to you on the day.

Availability of individual services is confirmed at the point of enquiry. We are a pre-launch service and we would rather confirm a scope than assume it.

Reporting portal and data protection

Studies are uploaded to a secure portal and reports are archived there. No certification or accreditation is claimed.

How the service works

Four steps, using the equipment and workflow your team already has.

1

Your facility acquires the study

The imaging is performed at your site, with your equipment and your protocol.

2

The study is uploaded

The study and its accompanying clinical information are uploaded to our secure portal.

3

A licensed radiologist interprets it

A reporting radiologist produces a structured report. Urgent and emergency studies are prioritised, and the modality and subspecialty route decide the reader.

4

The report is returned and archived

The report returns to your facility and is archived. Critical findings are notified to your named clinician, with the time of notification recorded.

Turnaround targets

These are published targets, measured from upload, not guarantees. Actual turnaround depends on study completeness and the reader bench at the time.

Target turnaround by priority, measured from upload.

Quality assurance: a documented share of studies is planned to be independently second-read each month, currently set at 5–10%. This is a planned policy, not a track record.

What comes back to your facility

Every study returns as a structured report through the portal, not a paragraph in an inbox.

Study identification

The facility, the identifiers your site supplies, the modality, the body region, and the date and time the study was performed.

Technique and comparison

What was performed, and which prior studies the current images were compared against.

Findings and impression

A systematic description by region, and the diagnostic impression written so a referring clinician can act on it. Mammography is reported using BI-RADS.

Critical findings and notification

Where a finding is critical, your named clinician is notified and the time of that notification is recorded.

Signature and time stamps

The reporting radiologist who finalised the report, and the time it was released to your facility.

Archive and retention

Reports are archived in the portal. The retention period is defined per agreement, typically 7–10 years.
